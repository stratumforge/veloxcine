Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 14, 2026 | Developer: StratumForge Labs™ (forgestratum@gmail.com)

1. Overview

VeloxCine™ ("we", "our", or "the extension") is developed by StratumForge Labs™. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy discloses what data is accessed, how it is used, and how your privacy is safeguarded.

2. Data Collection and Usage

VeloxCine operates on a privacy-first, client-side architecture:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not track, collect, store, or transmit your browsing history, media watch history, keystrokes, passwords, or personal communications.
  • Google Account (Gmail): If you purchase a Lifetime License, your signed-in Google account email is accessed locally via the Chrome Identity API (chrome.identity) strictly to verify your active purchase entitlement. Your email is never sold, shared, or used for unsolicited marketing.
  • Local Storage: Your preferences (subtitle styling, font size, dimmer levels, and aspect ratio choices) are stored locally on your device via chrome.storage.local.

3. Permissions Justification

  • Storage: Used solely to remember your custom subtitle and player preferences across browser sessions.
  • Tabs & Scripting: Used strictly to inject the video controller HUD and subtitle styling when you actively open supported streaming platforms.
  • Identity: Used exclusively to check your Lifetime Premium license entitlement linked to your Google Account.

4. Third-Party Services

VeloxCine does not sell data to data brokers, advertising networks, or unauthorized third parties. All video processing, transforms, and subtitle rendering happen entirely within your local browser sandbox.
